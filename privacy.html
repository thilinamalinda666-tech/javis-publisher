Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Who uses this app

Javis Publisher is a private tool used only by its owner to publish videos to the owner's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public.

What Google data it uses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app requests permission to upload videos (youtube.upload) and to read basic channel information (youtube.readonly), only to confirm it is posting to the right channel.

How the data is stored

The sign-in token is stored encrypted on the owner's own computer. It is not sent to any other service, sold, shared or used for advertising. No data about other people is collected.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
